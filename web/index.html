The platform

Everything exposed, in one continuously updated map

Discovery, validation and remediation in a single loop — so the findings that reach your queue are the ones that were actually reachable.

01

Continuous discovery

Cloud, on-prem, shadow IT and forgotten staging boxes are enumerated on a schedule and diffed against what you thought you owned.

02

Reachability analysis

A vulnerability that no one can reach is a backlog item, not an incident. We replay the real path before we rate the severity.

03

Chained attack paths

Single findings rarely matter. VulnLab connects the hops — leaked credential, exposed service, misconfigured trust — into the story an attacker would tell.

04

Scoped per team

Route findings to the service owner by repository, environment or tag, with service-level objectives attached to each one.

05

Evidence, not scores

Every finding ships with the request and response that proved it, so triage is a two-minute read instead of a reproduction task.

06

Integrates with your queue

Native sync with the trackers you already run. Findings arrive as tickets with context, not as a PDF nobody opens.
